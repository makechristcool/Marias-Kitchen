A rotating selection of Puerto Rican favorites, prepared fresh for your order. Planning a gathering? We'll help bring it all together.

THE CLASSICS

Arroz Amarillo

Yellow rice seasoned with sazón and sofrito, cooked low and slow.

A CROWD FAVORITE

Pernil

Slow-roasted pork shoulder, marinated overnight and roasted until tender.

THE HEART BEHIND THE FOOD

Every plate
has a story.

Maria learned to cook the way so many of us do—close to family, with a pot on the stove, sofrito in the air, and a little love in every recipe.
