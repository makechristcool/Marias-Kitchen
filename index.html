Our Story

From a Bronx kitchen to a Georgia table.

Maria learned to cook the way most of us do — standing on a stool next to family, stirring sofrito, arguing about how much sazón is too much sazón. She brought that kitchen from the Bronx down to Sugar Hill, and it hasn't lost an ounce of its energy.

Every pot of arroz con gandules, every slow-roasted pernil, still gets made the same way it always has: fresh, seasoned right, and made for sharing. We're building this as a catering business first, because we want to cook for your parties, your offices, your family reunions — and we're saving up for the truck that'll let us bring it to the street.

The Menu

What's cooking

A rotating spread of Puerto Rican staples, made fresh for every order. Ask about custom menus for your event.

Arroz Amarillo

Yellow rice, seasoned with sazón and a little sofrito, cooked low and slow.

Arroz Blanco

Simple white rice, done right — the base for everything else on the plate.

Pernil

Slow-roasted pork shoulder, marinated overnight and roasted until it falls apart.

Chicken Cutlets

Breaded chicken cutlets, fried golden and crispy on the outside, juicy inside.

Tostones

Green plantains, smashed and fried twice for that perfect crunch.

Maduros

Sweet ripe plantains, pan-fried until caramelized at the edges.

Habichuelas

Stewed beans, simmered in a rich sofrito-based broth. The soul of the plate.
